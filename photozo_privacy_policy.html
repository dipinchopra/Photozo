Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 18, 2026

At Blank Canvas Design Co., your privacy is our priority. This Privacy Policy explains how the Photozo App handles your data.

1. No Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or track any of the following:

  • Your personal information
  • Your usage behavior
  • The images you upload or generate
  • The text or prompts you enter

2. How We Process Your Data

The Photozo App uses large language models (LLMs) to generate images based on your inputs.

  • All generation requests are processed through secure third-party AI APIs (e.g., OpenAI).
  • Your data is used only to fulfill the request and is not saved by us.
  • We do not log or retain any prompts, results, or user metadata.

3. Third-Party Services

While we do not store or track any information, Photozo relies on AI APIs which may have their own privacy practices. Please refer to the relevant third-party provider’s privacy policy (e.g., OpenAI) for more information.

4. Children’s Privacy

The Photozo App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect or store data from minors.

5. Security

Although we do not store data, we take reasonable measures to ensure the secure transmission of requests between your device and the AI model.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If changes are made, we will update the "Last Updated" date above.
